QUICK STEPS — ZERO TO FIRST DISCIPLINED TRADE
  1. Install a broker app & finish KYC. India: Zerodha Kite · Groww · Upstox · Angel One. US: Robinhood · Webull · Interactive Brokers. Crypto: any regulated exchange app. Use paper/virtual mode first if it exists.
  2. Research HERE, not in the app. Press /, type the name (e.g. "reliance"), open it. Check the Signals strip, press AUTO PLAN, then CAPTURE → ANALYZE. Only a TAKE verdict (≥2R) goes further.
  3. Place the exact trade in the broker app. LIMIT buy at the analyzer's entry, stop-loss order at the stop, quantity exactly as the sizer says. If your app has bracket/cover orders — use them, they bind stop + target together.
  4. Set two alerts in the broker app: one at your stop, one at your target. Keep this site's Armed Trades open to monitor live P&L in R.
  5. Same night: log it in the Journal (one honest sentence). Weekly: review expectancy & win rate. Three straight losses → cut risk to 0.25% until you're green again.

Reading a candle — the vocabulary of price
Each candle compresses a time window (1m, 15m, 1 day…) into four numbers: open, high, low, close. Green = close above open. Long wicks mean rejection; fat bodies mean conviction.
Support & resistance — where stops actually belong
A stop placed just below a swing low (for longs) is invalidated only if the market truly breaks structure. That is the stop the Auto Plan — and every capture — computes for you.
The 1% rule — the exact math
Account ₹8,00,000 → you may lose ₹8,000 per trade. Stop ₹20 away → 400 shares. The sizer does this every time; the dial caps the whole day at 1%.
Think in R, not in currency
1R is whatever you risked. At 2R targets you only need to win ~40% of trades to grow. This is why the verdict engine rejects anything under 2:1.
Which timeframe is "best"?
There is no universal best — there is your goal's best. Same-day trades: 15m trend gate, 5m setup, capture on 1m. Swings (1–5 days): 1D for trend, 15m for entries. Investing: 1W context, 1D decisions. The MY PLAN tab picks this for you after four questions.
Short-term trading = time stops
"Withdraw within a short period" is a time constraint, so it needs a time rule: if the trade hasn't worked in N bars, exit flat and redeploy.
The four beginner traps
1) Averaging down a loser. 2) Moving the stop further away. 3) Overtrading past the daily 1% budget. 4) Not journaling. The checklist modal blocks all four.
